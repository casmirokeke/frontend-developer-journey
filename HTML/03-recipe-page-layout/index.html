🍚 Nigerian Jollof Rice

A simple and delicious Nigerian-style jollof rice recipe.

Recipe Information

Preparation time: 20 minutes

Cooking time: 45 minutes

Servings: 4 people

Ingredients

  • 2 cups of long-grain rice
  • 4 large tomatoes
  • 2 red bell peppers
  • 1 large onion
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 3 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon curry powder
  • 1 teaspoon thyme
  • 2 cups of chicken stock

Cooking Instructions

  1. Wash the rice thoroughly and set it aside.
  2. Blend the tomatoes, peppers, and onion into a smooth mixture.
  3. Heat the vegetable oil in a pot and sauté some chopped onion.
  4. Add the tomato paste and blended pepper mixture. Cook for about 15 minutes.
  5. Add the curry powder, thyme, and salt.
  6. Add the washed rice and chicken stock. Stir everything together.
  7. Cover the pot and cook on low heat for 30 minutes.
  8. Check that the rice is fully cooked, then serve hot.

Nutrition Note

Each serving contains approximately 350 kcal.

The recipe provides approximately 12g of protein per serving.

Water requirement: 2cups of stock.

Tips

  • Don't use too much water.
  • Cook the rice on low heat to prevent burning.
  • Allow the rice to rest for a few minutes before serving.
